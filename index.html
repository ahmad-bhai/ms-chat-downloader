    This FAQ answers the basic questions about MS Chat.
    What is MS Chat?

    MS Chat is a fast and secure messenger with its own cloud storage. You chat with people by username, no phone number needed.

    Do I need a phone number?

    No. You sign up and log in with just an email. Your email is never shown to other people.

    How do I create an account?

    Open MS Chat and tap Create new account. Enter your name, your email and, if you like, a username. Then tap Create account and your account is ready.

    How do I log in?

    On the login page choose Login with email or Login with QR.

    How do I log in with email?

    Choose Login with email, enter your email and tap Send code. A one-time code (OTP) appears on a device where you are already logged in. Enter it and tap Continue, then type your two-step verification password and tap Login. If two-step verification is off, you are logged in right away.

    How do I log in with QR?

    Choose Login with QR. On a device that is already logged in, open MS Chat, go to Settings > Privacy and Security > Devices > Add Device and scan the code. If you use two-step verification, enter it. Otherwise you are logged in directly.

    How do I set a profile photo?

    Go to My Profile > Set Photo and select a picture.

    How do I change my name, username, bio, birthday or email?

    Go to Settings > Account. There you can set or change your name, username, bio, date of birth and email. Tap Done when you finish.

    How do I secure my account?

    Open Settings > Privacy and Security. You can set up two-step verification and a passkey, change your email, manage blocked users and devices, choose who sees your last seen, and more.

    What can I send in a chat?

    Text, emojis in Telegram / Apple style, voice messages, photos, videos, audio and files. You can send and receive all media.

    Can I use more than one account?

    Yes. You can use up to 5 accounts at the same time.

    Can I customize the app?

    Yes. Switch between light and dark themes, set animations the way you like, make message text bigger or smaller, and change the chat wallpaper. There are 5 built-in wallpapers, and you can use a picture from your gallery or a link.

    Do I get notifications in the background?

    Yes. MS Chat sends notifications even when the app is closed.

    What is MS Chat cloud storage?

    MS Chat has its own cloud storage, so your chats and media stay available on all your devices.
